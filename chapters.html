Our Chapters

Local teams, open conversations

NeuroCove chapters bring mental health into the open in their own communities, from Nepal to Bergen County, New Jersey.

Nepal Chapter

Making mental health easier to talk about

NeuroCove Nepal is doing incredible work to make mental health a topic that people can openly talk about in a community where it is still often treated as taboo. Their team has worked tirelessly to create an environment to support these conversations.

They recently organized a large mental health event featuring 4 guest speakers and more than 50 participants. They have a movie watching event coming up to raise funds and grow their chapter.

Donate to Nepal @theneurocovefoundation.nepal

Bergen County Chapter

A new team in New Jersey

NeuroCove Bergen County (NJ) is a new team working to make mental health a topic that people can openly talk about and seek support for in their community. Their growing team is passionate about creating a welcoming space for meaningful conversations around mental health and building a community where people feel heard and supported.

They are planning upcoming events, so be sure to follow them on Instagram to stay updated and see what they have planned.

@bergenneurocovefoundation
